## Overview

Our team's submission to the 2023 High School Mathematical Contest
in Modeling (HiMCM), organised by COMAP.

We addressed Problem A, **“Dandelions: Friend? Foe? Both? Neither?”**,
investigating how dandelions spread across a one-hectare plot and
developing a framework for assessing plant invasiveness.

## Modelling approach

- **Differential equations:** model seed and mature-plant populations,
  accounting for reproduction, growth, mortality, resource competition
  and environmental conditions.
- **Cellular automaton:** investigate spatial growth and dispersal,
  including local resource availability and interactions between plants.
- **Invasiveness assessment:** combine a two-species competition model
  with the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) to construct a risk index,
  applied to dandelions, kudzu and tumbleweeds.

The report compares model behaviour under different assumptions and
discusses the strengths and limitations of each approach.
